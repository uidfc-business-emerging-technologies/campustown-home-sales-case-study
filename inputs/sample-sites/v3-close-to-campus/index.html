Data notes

  • Which sales: the 12,254 sales from March 26, 2025 to March 25, 2026, the most recent 12 months in the College-town home sales dataset (version 1.0). A buyer cares about current prices, so older sales are left out. Prices were fairly steady across the full three years of data, with quarterly medians between about $380,000 and $435,000.
  • Left out: 19 sales under $10,000 in those 12 months, which are not ordinary market sales (the lowest was $100).
  • Budget is compared with the sale price only. It doesn't include closing costs, property taxes or financing.
  • Distance is the straight-line distance (miles_to_campus) to one landmark on each campus, such as Foellinger Auditorium at Illinois. It is not travel time.
  • Size is the median finished living area of the homes within budget. Living areas under 100 square feet are treated as missing.
  • Days on market runs from the listing date to the contract date. Sales missing either date are left out of that figure, never counted as zero, and the share missing is shown.
  • All home types are included, because the data's home_type column is unreliable. Condo units that share a street address are separate homes, so none are removed as duplicates.
  • Small groups: a size or speed figure based on fewer than 20 sales is not shown.
  • The site computes every figure in your browser from data/sales.json, which scripts/build_data.py makes from the full dataset.

Data: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026.
