Data notes

  • Data: all sales in the College-town home sales dataset (version 1.0). Every figure is computed by scripts/build_data.py, which writes data/age.json; the page reads only that file.
  • Year built is as reported. sale has no year built and is left out of the year figures. homes are recorded as built in exactly 1900 and in 1901, against about a year from 1902 to 1909. Many of these are probably a default for "very old, year unknown". We keep them, since they are old homes either way, but read the 1900s decade with care.
  • Distance is miles_to_campus, the straight-line distance to a landmark on campus, in five bands: under 1 mile, 1 to under 2, 2 to under 5, 5 to under 10, and 10 or more.
  • Price per square foot is sale price divided by living area, for each sale of $1,000 or more with at least 100 square feet of living area ( sales). We take the median of those values. Sales under $1,000 and tiny or zero living areas are not real prices per square foot.
  • New home: built in the year it sold or the year before, or sold before it was finished ( homes have a year built later than their sale). Shares use the sales with both a year built and a sale date.
  • Sold without a listing means the sale has no list_date. The data description sheet says these are off-market sales or new homes sold by the builder.
  • Repeated addresses: we keep every sale, because condo units share street addresses.
  • Medians, not means, because a few sales are far from typical.
  • Small groups: a median based on fewer than 20 sales is not shown.
  • Not used: the home_type column, which the description sheet says is unreliable.

Data: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026.
