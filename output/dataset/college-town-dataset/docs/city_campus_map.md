# City to campus map

18 cities across 11 campuses. Assignment is by each property's coordinates, not its city label, so a row whose city field is wrong still lands in the right campus group.

Every city listed here appears in the dataset, and the row counts sum to all 35,442 rows. Cities with fewer than 90 sales were dropped from the dataset during the build.
